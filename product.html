What you'll notice.
And when.

Most longevity supplements ask you to wait three months on faith. We won't. There's a real, early signal — and a slower, structural change underneath. Both are measured. We'd rather you know which is which.

    What you'll feel first

    An early signal.

    Skin holds makeup differently. Looks brighter under indoor light. Feels smoother under your fingers. The barrier rehydrates first — and that changes how light reflects off your skin.

    The mechanismHyaluronic complex restores stratum-corneum hydration. Glutathione drops the immediate antioxidant load. Light reflectance shifts. Real, but it's the surface — not the structure.
  2. Week 4–8
    What others will notice

    A visible change.

    Tone evens out. Pigmentation drift starts to settle. The barrier holds water through the day instead of needing reapplication. People who haven't seen you in a month start asking.

    The mechanismTyrosinase regulation kicks in (glutathione, white tomato, niacinamide). TEWL drops measurably. Melanocyte activity quiets. Visible to the eye, measurable to the Corneometer.
  3. Week 12+
    What's happening underneath

    The longevity work.

    Dermal collagen density. Elasticity recovery. Cumulative oxidative defense. The part you don't see in the mirror — measured by Cutometer, 8-OHdG, collagen echo density. The part longevity science actually cares about.

    The mechanismCollagen peptide stimulates fibroblast Type I synthesis. Astaxanthin protects membrane lipid from peroxidation. Niacinamide feeds NAD⁺. Measured in months, not days.

Most ingestibles either chase the early signal (and lose all longevity credibility) or hide it (and lose all retention). We'd rather be honest about both. The early signal earns you the first week. The longevity work earns you the years.

Questions

The ones
we get most.

Full clinical reasoning on the Science page. SciCare team answers within one business day.

Why nine actives instead of one, dosed higher?+
Because skin ages along four parallel mechanisms — tone, barrier, structure, oxidative defense — and no single ingredient addresses more than one. There's no dose of glutathione that also restores ceramide. The 4×9 protocol gives each mechanism at least one dose-validated active, plus a glo-patented structure complex (Tightening-PB) that pairs with the collagen peptide on the firmness axis.
Why is the collagen dose only 100 mg?+
Because molecular weight — not gross intake — is the rate-limiting absorption variable. The collagen in glo is hydrolyzed to 300 Da from cod skin Type I, with a clean Pro-Hyp / Hyp-Gly dipeptide profile that crosses the intestinal epithelium intact. Ultra-low-MW peptides deliver meaningfully higher bioavailability per gram than bulk 5–10 g collagen powders, and the Tightening-PB Complex® (N°08) works in synergy on the firmness axis. Full rationale on the Science page.
What does "trial-form equivalent" mean?+
Each active in glo is sourced and specified to match the chemical form that the underlying clinical evidence used — reduced glutathione (not oxidized), Type I cod-skin collagen at ultra-low MW, natural Haematococcus-derived astaxanthin (not synthetic), and so on. Form discipline is inseparable from dose discipline; the same milligram in the wrong form has a very different in-vivo profile.
What is the Tightening-PB Complex®?+
A proprietary multi-ingredient botanical complex developed and patent-registered by glo (KR Patent No. 10-2911449). It sits inside the Dermal Structure pillar alongside the collagen peptide and targets skin firmness and dermal density — the part collagen alone doesn't fully carry. Dosed at 10 mg per shot.
Is this safe with my current prescriptions?+
For most people, yes — the actives in glo are well-tolerated and have a long evidence base. But if you're on anticoagulants (e.g. warfarin), oral isotretinoin, hormonal therapy, thyroid medication, or any hepatic CYP450 substrate, please consult your physician before starting. Email official@weglow.biz with specific drug-interaction questions and our science team will reply with the relevant literature.
When will I notice something?+
Hydration: typically measurable at weeks 4–6. Tone: visually noticeable around weeks 8–12. Firmness: Cutometer changes at 12 weeks. Oxidative defense markers (↓ 8-OHdG) are cumulative — the longevity part, not the mirror part.
What's the refund policy?+
90-day full refund, including used product, if you've taken the protocol as written and aren't seeing change. We'd rather refund you than have you on something that isn't working.
